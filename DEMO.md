# Swivel Sentio: 2-minute demo script

**Setup before judges arrive (about 2 minutes)**
- Laptop: app open at `http://localhost:3000`, press **Ctrl+F5**, sound on, **Reset demo to a
  calm state**.
- Phone: ntfy app subscribed, and **Emily's page** open (`/emily.html` on the public link). Check
  the green **Live** dot.
- Optional: **Enable camera** and **Use demo monitor** on Granddad's screen. Have one real scam
  text copied, in case a judge asks.

---

### 0:00 – The hook (15 sec)
> "Every scam against our grandparents works the same way: fear, urgency, secrecy. They pay
> before anyone they trust can say *wait*. Banks catch fraud after the money moves. We catch it
> **before**, at the moment of pressure. Our domain is the one question every victim should ask:
> **is this really you?**"

*Show Granddad's screen: the calm shield and "Feeling: Calm."*

### 0:15 – The scam call (30 sec)
*Family Portal → **IRS arrest call**.*
> "A fake IRS agent is threatening arrest unless Granddad wires $2,400. **Gemini** reads the call,
> scores it, and explains in plain English why it's a scam. If his face looks scared, or his heart
> races, the risk goes up even more."

*Granddad's screen takes over. Click **Listen to calming audio guide**.*
> "Instead of a scary alarm, an **ElevenLabs** voice calms him down. Notice it never repeats the
> scammer's threats back to him."

### 0:45 – Emily's phone (30 sec) ← the big moment
*The phone buzzes. Hold it up.*
> "His daughter Emily gets this on her real phone: the amount, the scam type, Gemini's reason,
> and that Granddad looks scared. Stopping money is **one tap**…"

*Tap **Veto** on the phone. Point at the laptop: it updates by itself to "payment stopped".*
> "…and Granddad's screen updates instantly. Approving takes **two deliberate taps** and never
> works from a lock screen, because releasing money should never be an accident."

### 1:15 – Texts and spoofing (25 sec)
*Reset → **Text message** → **Package fee**.*
> "Scams come by text too. Gemini catches this fake USPS fee as delivery-fee phishing."

*Turn on **Family check on every money request**, then **Family asks for $150**.*
> "And because a scammer can spoof a family member's number, families can require a check on
> *every* money request, even ones that sound normal."

### 1:40 – Memory and close (20 sec)
*Scroll to Protection history, and show Emily's phone history.*
> "Every check, safe or not, and every stress reading is stored in **Tiger Data** as time-series
> data, so families get a full audit trail that survives restarts."
>
> "Swivel Sentio: a quiet shield that adds a moment of friction exactly when scammers count on
> there being none."

---

### Likely judge questions
- **"Is the face reading accurate?"** It's an on-device experimental cue, not a diagnosis, and it
  only ever *raises* caution. Video never leaves the laptop.
- **"Is the heart rate real?"** It uses the standard Bluetooth heart-rate protocol, so real chest
  straps work. For the demo it's a clearly labeled simulated monitor. Presage's camera vitals SDK
  is the planned upgrade.
- **"What if Gemini is down?"** Offline keyword rules still catch the scams, and every service has
  a fallback, so the shield never fails open.
- **"Can it read my real texts?"** Websites can't. Today you paste or forward them; a native app
  is the next step.
- **"What does it cost to run?"** Gemini responses and voice clips are cached and capped. A day of
  demos used a tiny fraction of the free tiers.
